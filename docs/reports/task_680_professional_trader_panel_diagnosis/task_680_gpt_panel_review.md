# Task680 GPT Panel Review

## Review Setup

GPT was asked to simulate five professional trader perspectives:

- Systematic Quant Trader.
- Discretionary Growth/Momentum PM.
- Event-Driven Equity Analyst.
- Macro/Sector Rotation Trader.
- Portfolio Risk Manager.

## Consensus

- The project does not primarily lack information.
- It lacks a proper hierarchy for combining information.
- Current labels describe states, but they do not yet create strong rational prediction.
- The next work should not be another cap, filter, or blacklist.

## Panel Priorities

1. Winner Archetype Engine.
2. Same Symbol Divergence Matrix.
3. Leadership Lifecycle Panel.
4. Catalyst Quality Matrix.
5. Slot Qualification Study.

## Plain Conclusion

- The project has been too focused on finding losers.
- Active cap3 depends on preserving scarce high-upside opportunities.
- The next step is not to predict the exact future winner.
- The next step is to better separate setups that rationally deserve scarce top-five capital at entry.
